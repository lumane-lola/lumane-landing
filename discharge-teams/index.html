How it works

From "ready to leave" to "safely home."

  1. Step 01

    Know what they qualify for.

    Lumane checks the patient against public and community programs, including Medi-Cal and CalAIM Community Supports, and shows you what fits.

  2. Step 02

    Find the right providers.

    Lumane matches the patient with agencies and community organizations that serve their area, insurance, and needs.

  3. Step 03

    Send every referral.

    Lumane prepares each referral packet and sends it by email or fax. Providers don't need a login to receive it.

  4. Step 04

    Track until they're home.

    Every referral is followed up and its status kept in one place, so you can see what's confirmed and what still needs a push.

FAQ

Questions discharge teams ask us

01Does Lumane replace my judgment?

No. Lumane handles the searching, paperwork, sending, and follow-up. You decide what the patient needs and approve every referral before it goes out.

02Do the agencies I refer to need to sign up?

No. Providers and community organizations receive referrals by email or fax, the way they already do. There's nothing for them to install or log in to.

03How is patient information protected?

Lumane is built for protected health information. Patient data is used only to coordinate the patient's care, and every vendor that handles it is under a business associate agreement. Read our privacy policy.

04Where is Lumane available?

Lumane is live with skilled nursing facilities in California today, with hospitals and other states next. If you're elsewhere, request access anyway and we'll reach out when we launch near you.
